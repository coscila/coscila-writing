# Scope and input intake (Step 1)

English translation by OC Science Lab, 2026-09-09. Original licensing and attribution apply.

## Purpose

Use **a few questions** to establish the case boundaries before identifying patentable points and writing the disclosure. Missing information may be skipped, inferred later and **marked as an assumption**.

## Optional opening

```text
To identify patentable points and organize the disclosure, let us establish a few boundaries. You may skip these; I will infer from the materials available.
```

## Question sequence

### Q1: Technical subject or product module

```text
Describe the technical subject or product module in one sentence, for example “multimodal retrieval ranking” or “defect grading for industrial quality inspection”.
```

### Q2: Preferred patent claim type

```text
Do you already prefer a claim type: method, system, apparatus, or undecided?
```

### Q3: Technical contact in the document header

```text
Should the disclosure header include a technical contact? If so, provide a name, phone number and email; otherwise use “To be completed” for all three.
```

## Recap

Restate confirmed items in 3–6 bullet lines, then proceed to the scan described in `project_scan.md`.
