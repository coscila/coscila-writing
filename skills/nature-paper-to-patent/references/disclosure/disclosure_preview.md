# Summary preview before disclosure generation (Step 6)

English translation by OC Science Lab, 2026-09-09. Original licensing and attribution apply.

## Purpose

Reduce rework before writing a long document: present a structured summary and ask the user to **confirm or adjust the direction**.

If the user explicitly skips the preview, proceed directly to full drafting using `disclosure_builder.md`.

## Include

- Name of the selected patentable point, as a working title.
- The technical problems solved, corresponding to 1–3 shortcomings of existing technology.
- The central innovative modules or steps, in 3–6 items.
- One paragraph explaining differences from the **closest** retrieved prior art.

## Optional confirmation wording

```text
Is this direction confirmed? I will then use the template to produce the full technical disclosure, including Mermaid system and process diagrams. At finalization these will be rendered to PNG and delivered with both .md and .docx files.
```

## Do not append

Do not add footnotes at the end of the summary Markdown naming the skill repository, an `examples/` path, “teaching example”, “fictional”, or “not a legal commitment”. This matches the final-draft rule in `disclosure_builder.md`.
