# Identify and combine patentable points (Steps 3–4)

English translation by OC Science Lab, 2026-09-09. Original licensing and attribution apply.

## Step 3: Candidate patentable points

- List **3–5** candidate patentable points.
- For each, explain:
  - Technical background.
  - Innovation.
  - Differences from existing technology: these may initially be inferred from supplied material, then tightened in chapter 1 after prior-art searching.
  - Practical implementability.
- **Moderate extrapolation** from established facts is allowed when technically justified.

## Step 4: Combine and select

- Check whether the innovative elements of several points can be combined.
- Combine related technical points into a **method-and-system** patent when appropriate.
- Emphasize **combinatorial innovation**: a distinctive solution formed by an integrated combination of elements.

### Output strategy

**Default:** prioritize **one disclosure with the greatest value**.

If the user explicitly requests several, first give their outlines (title and central distinction), then agree on a drafting order.

### Selection criteria

- Novelty and grant potential.
- Differentiation from prior-art findings, rechecked after searching.
- A complete, implementable technical solution.
- Reasonable protection scope.
